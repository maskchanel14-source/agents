## 2. Disclosed simplifications

1. **"Two hundred years, one Italian family."** The 1765 report and the 1986 Venetian kindred are traditionally presented as the same lineage; that link is not documented in the primary literature. The film does not name a city and does not claim a continuous pedigree.
2. **Prion structure.** No experimentally determined structure of the infectious PrP^Sc conformer exists. The fibre on screen is a **stylised beta-stack**, not a molecular model. It is drawn to communicate "orderly, tightly packed, irreversible," and the HUD labels it as a schematic.
3. **Thalamic anatomy.** The two lobes are a schematic. The film says "thalamus," never a specific nucleus, because the exact nuclei involved differ between reports.
4. **EEG lanes.** The four traces (wake / spindles / deep / REM) are generated waveforms shaped to look like polysomnography. They are not recordings from any patient. The "FLAT" annotation is a didactic device for "this stage is absent."
