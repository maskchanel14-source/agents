For two hundred years, one Italian family could not sleep.
The cause was traced to a single letter of DNA.
One mutation in the gene for a prion protein.
That protein is in your brain right now.
When it misfolds, it grabs its neighbours and folds them too.
Your neurons end up holding a clump of protein that survives boiling water and bleach.
It gathers in the thalamus. A switchboard the size of a walnut that decides when you sleep.
Sleep spindles vanish first. Then deep sleep. Then the stage where you dream.
Patients stay awake for months. Heart racing, sweating, burning fuel they cannot store.
Half of every generation inherits it. And knows for decades before the first symptom.
Average survival: eighteen months. There is no treatment.
Only about seventy families on Earth. Zero survivors.
And it is not infectious. It is you.
