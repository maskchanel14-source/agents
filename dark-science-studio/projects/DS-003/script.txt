In December 2024, thirty-eight scientists published a warning. Not about a bomb. About a bacterium.
One that does not exist yet.
Every living thing on Earth is built the same way. Life has a handedness.
Sugars twist right. Proteins twist left. Your immune system reads that shape.
Now flip it. Build the same cell in mirror image.
Your antibodies would not grip it. Phages would not infect it. Antibiotics would not touch it.
It could eat the food you eat, and nothing on this planet could eat it.
The report says ten years, with serious funding.
Fifteen to thirty on the path we are on now.
They are not asking for a ban on knowledge.
They are asking us to never build it.
Thirty-eight scientists. One warning. Please.
