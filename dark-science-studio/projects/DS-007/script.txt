An amoeba swam up a boy's nose and started eating his brain.
It is Naegleria fowleri. Not a parasite, a free living amoeba.
It lives in warm fresh water, above thirty degrees Celsius.
You cannot catch it by drinking. Stomach acid destroys it.
It has to go up the nose, along the olfactory nerve, through a plate of bone, into the brain.
Then it multiplies, and the brain swells.
From first symptom to death: five point three days, on average.
The CDC has recorded one hundred sixty seven American cases since 1962.
Four people survived.
That is a fatality rate above ninety seven percent.
In 2016, a sixteen year old in Florida became one of them.
Salt water kills the amoeba. The ocean is safe.
But the lake is not. And it begins as a headache.
