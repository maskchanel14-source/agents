Status: **PREPARED, NOT APPROVED.** This is the only Level-7 asset, kept here for completeness of the escalation ladder. Default recommendation: **don't** — the gift-first 1:1 partnership path (see partnership-drafts) achieves most of this with a fraction of the risk.

## If the human ever approves a micro-batch, the compliant shape is:
- **Target source:** individually-researched public business contacts (e.g., coaches who publicly post a business email for inquiries) — never scraped lists, never purchased data.
- **Lawful basis:** legitimate interest with immediate opt-out (jurisdiction-dependent — human gets advice for target regions; honor CAN-SPAM/GDPR/PECR; business-contact rules differ in the UK/DE vs US).
- **Volume:** ≤20/day from a warmed, dedicated subdomain (never the main domain); SPF/DKIM/DMARC aligned.
- **Template (value-first, personalized line mandatory):**
  > Subject: Your [specific page] — one 5-second-test observation
  > Hi [Name] — I build a free landing-page clarity checker. Looking at [their actual page], [one genuine, specific observation + the fix]. If useful, the full free score is here: [link ?ref=outreach]. If not, no follow-up from me. — [Real name, real business, postal address, unsubscribe line]
- **Personalization floor:** the bracketed observation must be true and page-specific (human verifies each). No mail-merge fake personalization ("Love your work!!").
- **Opt-out:** one-click + reply-based; suppression list honored forever; no re-adding.
- **Expected metric:** replies + scans per 100 sends; kill instantly on first spam complaint pattern or <1% positive replies after 100.
- **Risk assessment:** MEDIUM-HIGH (deliverability + brand + legal variance). Risk owner: human. Fallback if rejected: partnership gift-first path + community drafts (already prepared).
