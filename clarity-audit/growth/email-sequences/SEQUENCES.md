# Email Sequences — DRAFTS (opt-in only; human loads into provider)

Rules baked into every draft: sent only to explicit opt-ins · unsubscribe in every email · no fake urgency/reply-bait subjects · plain-text friendly · SPF/DKIM/DMARC before first send (human).

## Sequence A — Post-scan (trigger: scan-result opt-in)
**A1 (immediate) — "Your clarity result + top 3 fixes"**
Subject: Your Clarity Score: {{score}}/100 — here are your 3 fixes
Body: score + grade, top-3 issues as a checklist (fix text included), link to result page, one line: "Fix #1 first — it's your quick win." Soft PS: sample of the paid full report if they want rewrites done for them. No countdown, no pressure.

**A2 (Day 2) — "The 5-second test, explained"**
Subject: Why 5 seconds decides your page
Body: methodology in plain English, link /methodology, invitation to try Headline Checker. Trust-building, zero pitch except footer link.

**A3 (Day 4) — Paid audit offer (honest)**
Subject: Want the rewrites done for you?
Body: what Full Audit adds (rewrite options, FAQ draft), $39 one-time, link to sample report FIRST, refund policy in one line. "If the free fixes were enough, ignore this proudly."

**A4 (Day 7) — Rescan reminder**
Subject: Fixed your headline? Let's see the new score
Body: rescan link, "share your improvement" prompt, badge explainer. (This is the viral loop's kindest form.)

**A5 (Day 14) — Template Kit (only if A3 not purchased)**
Subject: Steal these section templates ($49 kit)
Body: one free template inline (hero formula), kit contents, honest who-shouldn't-buy line.

## Sequence B — 5-Day Clarity Challenge (trigger: challenge opt-in)
Day 0 welcome ("Tomorrow: see your page like a stranger") · Days 1–5 mirror /challenge (scan → headline → CTA → FAQ → rescan+share), each ≤300 words with that day's template and ONE link. Day 6 wrap: badge celebration + referral invite (reward only after a friend's valid paid order — state terms plainly) + soft audit offer.

## Sequence C — Post-purchase (trigger: paid order webhook)
C0 delivery (immediate): report link + doc attached/hosted, what to do first, support contact.
C1 (Day 3): "Stuck on Day 2's fix?" help offer (Fix Pack support window) — service, not sales.
C2 (Day 8): rescan + testimonial request — ask only after value moment; include "say no freely" line; never incentivize positive wording (discount-for-review corrupts proof; a thank-you credit for *honest* feedback either way is the max).
C3 (Day 21): Template Kit / multi-page upsell, one email, no sequence nagging.

## Sequence D — Data study list
Single launch email when batch PDF publishes + optional quarterly update. Nothing else — promise kept = trust.

## Abandoned-scan/cart
Only for consented emails already captured: one "your result is here" reminder (A1 doubles as this). No browse-abandonment dark patterns, no fake "your discount expires" unless real.
