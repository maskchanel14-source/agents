A FEW STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before you say yes.

Can I buy it now?

No. This is a validation page. We have not built the full generator or verified a payment path. The form records a price-specific expression of interest, not an order.

Is this a legal contract or e-signature service?

No. The proposed output is a plain-language change record for your review. It does not provide legal advice, verify authority to approve, or guarantee payment. Keep your own service agreement.

Why not use a free template?

You can, and that may be the right choice. The hypothesis is that a focused, private workflow saves enough time on repeated changes to justify $19. We need actual buyers to test that.

What happens if there is not enough interest?

We stop. The experiment has a 48-hour hard stop from creation while distribution is being arranged. We will not claim market rejection if we never reached the audience, and we will not add features to hide a failed test.
