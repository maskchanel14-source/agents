# Source archive manifest

## Included in the portable source archive
- Remotion composition, timeline and R3F/Three scene source.
- `package.json` and `package-lock.json` for reproducible dependency installation.
- Procedural audio generator and the rendered original WAV music/SFX files.
- Arena-generated narration MP3 produced in this session.
- The local species still used in the evidence card, with attribution in `PUBLISH-KIT.md`.
- Render bootstrap script for this sandbox (`scripts/remotion-env.sh`), plus production plan, research log and publishing kit.

## Explicit exclusions
- `node_modules/`, build cache, browser extraction cache, render temp directories and QC scratch renders.
- No credentials, API tokens or hidden configuration.
- No third-party stock footage is included. A legal CC-licensed generic mushroom timelapse was identified in research, but the sandbox host allowlist prevented downloading its video binary. The final MP4 therefore does **not** label any still or visualization as footage.

## Legal notes
- The species still is a CC BY 4.0 work and requires attribution + a licence link; see publishing-kit table.
- The narration asset is included for the project owner only; follow the voice platform’s terms when reusing it.
- The cover base is generated later and must retain its AI-illustration disclosure.
