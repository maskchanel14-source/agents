## Description — ready to paste
Reports from southwest China and the northern Philippines describe miniature figures moving around real objects after people consumed a wild mushroom. In 2026, DNA work linked mushrooms from both places to *Lanmaoa asiatica*.

The unsettling part is also the limit: genome mining found no canonical biosynthetic genes for psilocybin or ibotenic acid, and the active chemical has **not** been isolated. That makes this a scientific lead—not a newly identified drug or mechanism. Historical reports from Papua New Guinea were not genetically verified in the 2026 work.

Do not taste or experiment with wild mushrooms. Undercooked wild mushrooms can cause poisoning; seek urgent medical advice after a suspected ingestion.
