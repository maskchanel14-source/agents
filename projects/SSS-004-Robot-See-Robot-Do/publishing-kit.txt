SMALL SIGNAL STUDIO — SSS-004: ROBOT SEE ROBOT DO

Suggested title: This Robot Copies What It Sees — But There's a Catch

Description:
A UC Berkeley team showed how a robot can imitate articulated object motion from a single demonstration video — with an important extra input: a multi-view scan of the object. Their method reconstructs part motion and uses it to plan a robot's actions. It is sensitive to camera viewpoint, occlusion, scan quality, and segmentation; it is not a universal robot trained from arbitrary videos.

Paper: Justin Kerr et al., Robot See Robot Do: Imitating Articulated Object Manipulation with Monocular 4D Reconstruction, CoRL 2024.
https://openreview.net/forum?id=2LLu3gavF1
