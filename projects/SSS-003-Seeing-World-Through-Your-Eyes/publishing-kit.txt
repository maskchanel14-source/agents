2. YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION (FIRST 2 LINES HOOK + CITATIONS + 4 HASHTAGS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every portrait video or selfie hides a sub-millimeter reflection on your wet cornea. Researchers at the University of Maryland trained a 3D Neural Radiance Field (NeRF) to step inside your eye and reconstruct the room in front of you.

Across healthy adults, the human cornea curves with a remarkably consistent ~7.8 mm radius dome (eccentricity e = 0.50), turning every eye into a tiny wide-angle curved mirror:

• Cornea Pose Optimization: Locks onto sub-millimeter eyeball rotation across portrait frames via SE(3) ray reflection geometry.
• 2D Iris Texture Decomposition: Simultaneously learns and peels away your colored iris pigment map so faint off-camera reflections (<5% of pixel energy) emerge in 3D.
• Scientific Reality & Limits: Reconstructs 3D rooms, toys, and off-camera objects when given sufficient resolution and head motion (4 or fewer frames or near-zero motion still causes geometry holes).
• Archival Music Video Test: Applied to existing close-up music videos of Miley Cyrus and Lady Gaga, recovering 3D silhouettes of studio softbox rigs and camera operators standing behind the lens years earlier.
