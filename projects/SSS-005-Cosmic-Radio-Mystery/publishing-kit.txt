SMALL SIGNAL STUDIO — SSS-005: FAST RADIO BURSTS

Suggested title: The Cosmic Signal That Disappears in Milliseconds

Description:
Fast radio bursts are fleeting flashes of radio waves, and some come from galaxies far beyond the Milky Way. A 2020 radio burst from a Galactic magnetar helped connect at least some FRB-like events to magnetars. For FRB 20221022A, a 2025 Nature study found evidence of a compact emission region near a neutron star. What creates the radio flashes in detail, and whether all bursts share the same type of source, remain open questions.

Main paper: Kenzie Nimmo et al., “Magnetospheric origin of a fast radio burst constrained using scintillation”, Nature 637, 48–51 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-08297-w
