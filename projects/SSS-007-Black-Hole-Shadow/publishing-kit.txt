SSS-007 — The Black Hole Picture Isn't What You Think

Suggested title: The Black Hole Picture Isn't What You Think
Description: That dark center in the first image of M87* is its shadow—not a direct picture of the event horizon. Here's what the Event Horizon Telescope actually showed.
