SSS-006 — WHEN THE SUN BLACKED OUT A GRID

Suggested title: The Solar Storm That Shut Down a Power Grid

Description:
On March 13, 1989, a geomagnetic storm contributed to the collapse of the Hydro-Québec power grid, leaving millions without power for around nine hours. Why can a disturbance from the Sun affect infrastructure on Earth? This Short explains the mechanism and its limits. Not every solar storm causes a blackout.

The footage is illustrative, not footage of the 1989 blackout: solar images show other dates and auroras show other locations. NASA SDO views are colourized extreme ultraviolet observations shown in accelerated time. The solar-cycle mosaic is a science time-lapse, not a real-time movie. No artificially generated visuals were used. Editing includes trimming, colour and scale changes, defocused background extensions, typography and new sound. Narration is synthesized; subtitles are approximately timed and require a final listening review. NASA does not endorse this video.
