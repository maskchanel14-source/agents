# V2 Style Spec — Hyper Documentary / Dark Science

## Motion language
- Hard cuts on 120 BPM half-bars/bars; an accented camera move begins immediately after every cut.
- Whip/throw accents only at factual turns; no default crossfades.
- Base footage has a deliberate push, lateral move and tiny organic micro-drift. It must never sit still for a second.
- Motion graphics are flat evidence marks—nodes, a line, labels, and a question mark—not fake molecule or hallucination reconstructions.

## Type system
- **Display + captions:** Space Grotesk Variable, never browser default hero text.
- **Metadata:** IBM Plex Mono.
- Captions are sentence case, two to four words per thought, bottom-safe, with a restrained lime active-word underline. Large all-caps is reserved for fewer than six story hits.

## Picture finish
- Dark forest base, acid-lime evidence accent, warm amber exception. Grain, vignette and subtle clarity/contrast enhancement sit above footage.
- The source media is generic mushroom footage. The disclosure remains present but subordinate.

## Sound
- Original 120-BPM dark pulse score: audible in pauses, ducked under narration.
- Whooshes precede picture cuts. Sub hit lands on major factual reveals; ticks support the DNA/unknown data hits.
- The exported mix targets social loudness after final render, without clipping.
