# Drafts v2, written to Harvey's writer rules

Rewritten 29 October 2026 after the audit. Every draft passes
`pre_send_gate.py`, which runs Harvey's own `pre_send_check` plus the rules from
`prompts/writer.md` that its gate does not enforce.

Changes from v1, all forced by the gate:

- 180 to 206 words became 45 to 60. Rule 10 says under 75 for email 1.
- Two questions became one. Rule 13.
- No link in email 1. The frameworks file says zero links is better, because link
  tracking costs 8 to 12 percent of inbox placement.
- The studio name appears once, in the signature. The product is never named.
- Vague CTAs ("worth a look", "interested") replaced with questions a person can
  answer in four words.

Signature block is deliberately flat: value, the two lines that matter, opt-out.
No trackable URL anywhere, so attribution now comes from asking, not from a query
string. That is the trade the data recommends.
