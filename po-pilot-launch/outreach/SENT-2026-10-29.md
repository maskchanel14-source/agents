## Reply handling

First reply always confirms scope, price and date in writing before asking for a
file. Never quote a delivery date before seeing the export, because the size of
the job is set by how clean that export is.

If they say yes and send data:

1. Move their row in `audit_requests` to `quoted`, or add one if they arrived by
   email rather than the form.
2. Ask them to strip customer names, emails and addresses. None of it is needed.
3. Deliver the vendor set first, not the whole catalogue. Small first win, then
   quote the rest.
