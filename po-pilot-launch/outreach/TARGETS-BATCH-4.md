## Why this batch is the same shape as the pitch

Ten are ecommerce accounting firms and ten are 3PLs or fulfilment providers, in
both cases because they already own the client relationship and the file. The
pitch asks them one thing: who is rebuilding purchase order history now that
Stocky is gone. Nothing is sold in the first email.

## Rejected, and why

- **bookskeep** (Cyndi Thomason): contact form and phone only, no published address.
- **ECOM CPA**: Calendly and a form, no published address.
- **Bean Ninjas**: first-name-only format published by data brokers, no generic
  address on their site. Guessing `hello@` is exactly what the policy forbids.
- **The Cozy Bookkeeper**: address appears on Nextdoor, not on their own site.
- **PIE Accountancy**: format inferred by third parties, nothing published.
- **Ecom CFO**, **eFulfillment Service**, **ShipMonk**: no self-published generic
  address found.

## Skip conditions kept from batch 3

Do not send where the domain fails MX, where the only address is a form, or where
the firm is a software product rather than a service (they sell the replacement,
not the relationship).
