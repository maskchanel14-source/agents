What actually replaces Stocky for purchase orders?

Honest note: if your team needs one shared screen to look at every morning, or you want a vendor to answer the phone, a subscription app is the right call. PO Pilot is for the buyer who already knows the rules and wants the arithmetic done correctly, once, without a monthly bill.

How to choose in five minutes

Stay on native POs if…

  • You run fewer than ~50 SKUs with 1–3 suppliers
  • You order in whole cartons anyway, so rounding is a non-issue
  • Nobody has ever disputed a supplier invoice on price

Rebuild the rules in a sheet if…

  • Your sourcing is seasonal and irregular
  • You enjoy owning the model and want it auditable
  • You only need it reviewed once a month

Pay for a subscription app if…

  • More than two people touch purchasing
  • You need forecasting and approvals in one UI
  • The team will not adopt anything that looks like a script

Use PO Pilot if…

  • You have the vendor rules and want them applied exactly
  • You already work inside Claude / Cursor / n8n
  • The invoice audit matters more than the dashboard

The part most comparisons skip: the data deadline

Stocky kept vendor lead times, pack multiples and MOQ history inside its own purchase order records. There was never a “download suppliers” button. Anything you do not export while the read-only window is open has to be reconstructed later from invoices, by hand.

That is why the first deliverable here is not software: it is a 12-point export checklist. Run it whether or not you ever buy anything — it is the only step with an expiry date.
