How it runs

No discovery phase, no scope creep, no access to your Shopify admin.

  1. You request and I confirm scope by email — usually within one business day. I quote the tier, you approve, invoice goes out (card or bank transfer).
  2. You send four files — Stocky purchase order export, products/inventory export, sales by SKU (90–180 days), and your supplier list (a screenshot is fine). Share them however you like; if you prefer, delete the customer columns first.
  3. I do the work offline — deterministic calculations, not a chatbot guessing at your numbers. Every figure in the report is reproducible from your CSVs.
  4. You get the deliverables — the rules file, the Draft PO CSV, the findings report, and a call. You own all files; nothing is held hostage behind a subscription.
  5. Optional — if you want the recurring version, the $49 kit does the same math monthly, or I can set up the workflow for you under the $750 tier.

Who this is for — and who it is not for

Good fit

50–2,500 SKUs · 3–30 suppliers · someone still builds purchase orders in a spreadsheet · you have at least a few months of Stocky PO history to work from · you can name one invoice you suspect was overbilled.

Not a good fit

Under ~50 SKUs with one supplier (use the free checklist, it will take you 20 minutes). Anyone who needs a dashboard their whole team can log into. Anyone who needs accounting, tax or legal advice. Anyone who wants me to write to their live store — I never get write access.
