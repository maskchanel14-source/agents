What actually broke on Aug 31

Stocky wasn't just a stock counter. It quietly held the purchasing rules your buyer relies on every Monday morning. When it went read-only, three things walked out the door:

Gap 1

Vendor rules lived in Stocky

Shopify's migration guide states plainly that suppliers can't be exported from Stocky. Lead times, pack multiples and MOQs lived only inside purchase order history.

Gap 2

Admin POs are a filing cabinet

Shopify's native Purchase Orders store drafts, but they don't compute stockout-adjusted velocity, don't round to case packs, and don't respect a vendor's dollar minimum.

Gap 3

Nobody audits the invoice

A silent $0.35/unit creep plus a short-shipped carton is a four-figure loss per quarter — and it never shows up in a spreadsheet review.

Four deterministic tools. No LLM math.

Every number is computed in code, not guessed by a model — penny-exact to the cent, so it survives an accountant's review.

migrate_stocky_csv_snapshot

Drop in your exported Stocky purchase order CSV. Reconstructs each vendor's real lead time (mean + std-dev from Created→Received), infers carton pack multiples from order history, and captures the latest unit cost per SKU.

Output: supplier rules config you can keep forever, even after the export window closes.

build_supplier_purchase_orders

Stockout-adjusted daily velocity, Z-score safety stock, ABC revenue tiers, carton rounding, MOQ / free-freight top-ups, US tariff + freight landed cost, and optional Open-to-Buy cash rationing.

Output: Shopify Draft PO CSV + POS transfer CSV (SKU, Barcode, Quantity) + supplier email drafts.

reconcile_po_three_way_match

PO vs dock receiving vs supplier invoice, line by line. Catches price creep, short-ships, unapproved SKUs and freight overruns, then drafts the vendor debit memo.

Output: variance audit + verified-only QuickBooks Online & Xero bill CSVs.

calculate_replenishment_plan

Reorder points and days-of-cover from a CSV or an optional read-only Shopify Admin token. Runs entirely in demo mode if you don't want to touch live store data.

Output: ranked stockout risk list with the cash value of each at-risk SKU.

Straight answers

Do I need a Shopify API key?
No. CSV mode works with a Stocky PO export or a Shopify products_export.csv. If you do want live data, an optional read-only token (read_products, read_inventory, read_orders) is enough — PO Pilot never writes to your store and never moves money.
Does it send my data anywhere?
In local mode, no: it runs on your machine and reads files you paste or point it at. The hosted Cloud plan runs on a managed endpoint instead, for teams that don't want to install Node or Docker.
Will it work with multi-location inventory?
Yes — the plan accepts per-location inventory, and the POS transfer CSV is generated per origin/destination pair. If your location naming is unusual, the parser handles custom headers.
My accountant uses QuickBooks Online / Xero, not Excel.
The 3-way match outputs bill CSVs in the exact column order those importers expect, containing only verified received quantities at contracted PO costs — so unapproved surcharges never reach your AP ledger.
What if it doesn't fit my workflow?
The engine is MIT and readable before purchase — test it free first. If you buy the kit and it genuinely doesn't run, reply to the receipt email within 14 days and you'll get a refund.
Why is the price one-time instead of a subscription?
Because the math doesn't change month to month. Subscription pricing for a calculator is how the $99–$249/month replacement apps justify themselves; a deterministic tool doesn't need a meter.
