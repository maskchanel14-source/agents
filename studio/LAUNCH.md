## Proposed r/sideprojects project post (not published)

**Title:** Would you pay $19 for a private change-order writer, or is a template enough?

**Body:**

I'm testing a small tool for solo web designers handling fixed-fee client projects. The moment it addresses: the client approves a page, then asks for another section or feature, and you need to price the change without writing the same awkward email again.

The proposed product, ScopeReply, would turn your original deliverable and the new request into a client-ready email and a printable change record. You enter the price and schedule impact yourself. It offers three clear choices: pay for the addition, swap something of equal scope, or move it to phase two.

The planned price is $19 once, not a subscription. The client-request text stays in your browser. No client account, AI credits, or e-signature workflow.

Here is a **fictional example**, not a customer result:

> Thanks for the new FAQ idea. Our agreed scope includes the five-page site and one contact form; an expandable FAQ with CMS editing is an addition. I can add it for $240 and two business days, swap it for the planned resources section at no extra charge, or keep it for phase two. I'll wait for your written choice before starting the extra work.

I make this tool, so this is product research, not an independent recommendation. A free template may already be enough, and I'd rather learn that before building it. If you handle fixed-fee web projects, would you actually pay $19 for this? What is missing that would make it useful on your next change request?

Validation page: https://scope-reply.vercel.app (production GET verified 2026-10-28; no post published)

No charge today. The page records interest in the $19 offer; it is not a checkout or a binding purchase agreement.
